Kitty Keys settings

Sites, presets and the ticker pattern are all data. {TICKER} = upper case, {ticker} = lower case, {q} = as typed. Add #element-id to a URL to land scrolled. Preset names and site keys double as omnibox words: k GME rk, k fv GME.

Profiles

A profile is a ready-made set of sites and presets. Replace swaps your config for it; Merge adds its sites and presets to yours.
